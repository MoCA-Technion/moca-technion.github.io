## What we do

MoCA develops **scalable, theory-grounded models** and **computational tools** for mobility systems and infrastructure analytics. We care about three things:

- **Scalability** (real networks, real datasets)
- **Flexibility** (rich behavior, interactions, heterogeneity)
- **Reliability** (theory-consistent and interpretable, not just predictive)

---

## Mobility systems

### Perturbed utility frameworks (static + dynamic)
Many mobility decisions can be represented as routing or sequential decision-making on networks. We study perturbed utility formulations that:
- remain computationally tractable at large scale,
- capture flexible substitution / interaction patterns,
- support estimation from disaggregated trajectory data.

A major direction is extending perturbed utility models to **dynamic settings** through Markovian choice models (MDP-based formulations).

### Supply–demand interactions & market design
We study endogenous interaction between demand and supply in on-demand services and platform ecosystems (e.g., ride-sharing, MaaS), including equilibrium modeling, matching, incentives, and regulation.
