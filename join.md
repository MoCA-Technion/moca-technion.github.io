MoCA Lab is always interested in motivated students and researchers who are passionate about advancing mobility systems through rigorous computational methods and data-driven insights.

## Open Positions

We have **full funding available** for positions in the following research areas:

- **Behavioral foundations** — Modeling how travelers and platforms make decisions, including preferences, trade-offs, constraints, and patterns of substitution and heterogeneity
- **Dynamic decisions and adaptation** — Studying sequential decision-making, repeated interactions, adaptive behaviors, and dynamic system feedback
- **Information and guidance** — Understanding how travelers respond to guidance, how guidance affects network conditions, and how to evaluate interventions from a public-interest perspective
- **Equilibrium, stability, and system design** — Analyzing demand–supply equilibrium, pricing and incentive mechanisms, multimodal system integration, and conditions for stability and robustness
- **Human–AI mobility ecosystems** — Exploring how digital tools and automated systems influence mobility choices, how human and algorithmic decision-makers coexist, and the opportunities and challenges of emerging technologies

Our work integrates **choice modeling**, **network modeling**, **optimization, machine learning**, and **policy analysis** to build models, algorithms, and decision tools that help society design transportation systems that are efficient, fair, and truly human-centered.

## What We Value

At MoCA Lab, we value:

- **Strong quantitative foundations** — Deep understanding of optimization, probability, machine learning, and network theory
- **Theory, applicability, and relevance** — Care about theoretical rigor, scalable to real-world applications, and societal relevance
- **Integration of perspectives** — Combining behavioral modeling, network modeling, and policy analysis to understand mobility systems holistically
- **Collaboration** — Working together to solve challenging problems
- **Innovation** — Developing new methods and approaches that go beyond the state-of-the-art

Our research focuses on questions that matter to policy makers, transport agencies, mobility platform operators, and travelers. We aim to help build mobility systems that work **for people and society**, not only for algorithms or market forces.
