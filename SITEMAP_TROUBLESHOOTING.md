# Sitemap Troubleshooting Guide

## Issue: "Sitemap could not be read" in Google Search Console

### ✅ Your Sitemap is Actually Working!

Your sitemap is accessible at: `https://moca-technion.github.io/sitemap.xml`

The issue is likely with **how you're submitting it** to Google Search Console.

## 🔧 How to Fix

### Step 1: Submit the Sitemap Correctly

**IMPORTANT**: When submitting to Google Search Console, use **ONLY** the filename, not the full URL:

❌ **WRONG**: `https://moca-technion.github.io/sitemap.xml`
✅ **CORRECT**: `sitemap.xml`

**Steps:**
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Select your property: `https://moca-technion.github.io`
3. Go to **Sitemaps** (left sidebar)
4. In the "Add a new sitemap" field, enter: `sitemap.xml`
5. Click **Submit**

### Step 2: Verify Sitemap is Accessible

Before submitting, verify your sitemap is accessible:
- Open: https://moca-technion.github.io/sitemap.xml
- It should display as XML (not an error page)

### Step 3: Check for Common Issues

#### Issue 1: Wrong URL Format
- ✅ Use: `sitemap.xml` (just the filename)
- ❌ Don't use: `https://moca-technion.github.io/sitemap.xml`

#### Issue 2: Sitemap Not Yet Generated
- Wait a few minutes after deploying to GitHub Pages
- The sitemap is auto-generated by the `jekyll-sitemap` plugin

#### Issue 3: Caching Issues
- Clear your browser cache
- Try accessing the sitemap in an incognito/private window
- Wait 5-10 minutes after deployment for CDN cache to update

#### Issue 4: Robots.txt Blocking
- Your `robots.txt` is correctly configured ✅
- It includes: `Sitemap: https://moca-technion.github.io/sitemap.xml`

### Step 4: Validate Sitemap Format

Your sitemap should be valid XML. You can validate it using:
- [XML Sitemap Validator](https://www.xml-sitemaps.com/validate-xml-sitemap.html)
- [XML Validator](https://validator.w3.org/)

### Step 5: Wait and Check Status

After submitting:
1. Wait 5-10 minutes
2. Refresh the Sitemaps page in Google Search Console
3. Check the status - it should show "Success" (green checkmark)

## 🆘 If Still Not Working

### Option 1: Remove and Resubmit
1. In Google Search Console → Sitemaps
2. Click the three dots next to your sitemap
3. Click "Remove"
4. Wait 1 minute
5. Submit again with: `sitemap.xml`

### Option 2: Test Sitemap URL
1. Go to **URL Inspection** tool in Google Search Console
2. Enter: `https://moca-technion.github.io/sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Test Live URL**
4. Check if Google can access it

### Option 3: Check for Errors
1. In Google Search Console → Sitemaps
2. Click on your sitemap
3. Check for any error messages
4. Look at "Coverage" tab for issues
