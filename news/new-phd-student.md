We are thrilled to announce that a new PhD student has joined the MoCA Lab! This addition strengthens our research team and brings fresh perspectives to our work on mobility systems and infrastructure analytics.

## Welcome to the Team

The new member brings expertise in computational methods and will be working on exciting projects related to:

- **Mobility Systems**: Developing scalable models for transportation networks
- **Data Analytics**: Applying machine learning techniques to infrastructure monitoring
- **Optimization**: Creating efficient algorithms for large-scale network problems

## Research Focus

The student will be collaborating with our team on several ongoing projects, contributing to our mission of advancing next-generation mobility systems through theory-grounded computational tools.

We look forward to the innovative research contributions they will bring to the lab!
